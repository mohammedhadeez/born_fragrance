## LOD assessment

LOD is the reliability of an individual model element, rather than a measure of how busy a sheet looks. BIMForum's 2025 LOD Specification, Part I, printed page 17, defines LOD 350 around measurable quantity, size, shape, location, orientation **and interfaces with adjacent/dependent elements**, supporting construction coordination. More linework alone does not establish LOD 350.

This package is a presentation made from native PowerPoint geometry and a Python-generated orthographic cutaway. It is not a delivered BIM model. Approved envelopes, member sizes and positions support dimensional coordination. Manufacturer hardware, fixing substrates, services and several connections are still unresolved; a blanket LOD 350 claim would overstate the available information.

## Axonometric development

The former heavy wire/block diagram is replaced with depth-tested edges, actual 3 mm trays and 25 downstands, secondary supports, mesh, neutral cabinet panels, indicative perfume silhouettes and the revised counter. Both storefront columns and near-side upper joinery use the same 600 cut datum. Native PowerPoint leaders use projection coordinates from the renderer, so their dots touch the named component. The ceiling tracks are displayed as an exploded layer with dashed projection guides; their installed datum remains 2600. The diagram is NTS, with no dimension inferred from its raster image or the original render. Wall and floor surfaces and counter panel split are graphic representations, not surveyed build-ups or cutting schedules.
